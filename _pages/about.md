My name is Jinguo Sun. I am an Alexander von Humboldt postdoctoral fellow hosted by Reactive Flows and Diagnostics (RSM) at Technische Universität Darmstadt (TUDa), Germany.

I received my bachelor's degree in June 2017 and Ph.D. in June 2022 from the Department of Energy and Power Engineering at Tsinghua University, Beijing, China.

My current work focuses on

- Plasma-assisted ammonia combustion
- Non-equilibrium plasma physics
- Advanced laser diagnostics
